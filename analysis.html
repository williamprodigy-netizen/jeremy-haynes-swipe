What they do, why it works, and what is worth taking.

The one thing worth stealing. Urgency video first, information videos second. He claims a single urgency video at the top of a confirmation page moved a webinar show rate 19 points, and that 19 information videos added only 4 more. Our confirmation page currently does neither — it confirms, and that is all it does.

Proof, and what kind it is

Operational, not aspirational

0.37% lifetime Stripe dispute rate over 7,241 transactions. 3–4% annualised churn. These are numbers that only exist if the business is real, and they are shown live on screen rather than as a graphic.

Chat screenshots, not video testimonials

Almost all social proof is screenshots of the private community and DMs — “$5000 for this level of information is just insane”, “bought your 5k offer Friday”, a member reporting $3,300 of ad spend returning $48,044. Text screenshots read as unstaged in a way a filmed testimonial does not.

The price is inside the proof

He never puts a price slide up. The number arrives through other people's mouths in screenshots. By the time he says $5,000 out loud, three testimonials have already said it for him.

No guarantee at all

Not a conditional one, not a 30-day one. Nothing. The dispute rate is doing the job a guarantee normally does.

Worth taking

Put an urgency video on the confirmation page

Not a reminder video. A real reason this specific class matters this specific week. He claims 19 points of show rate off one video and the ordering rule is explicit: urgency first, information second.

Ship the didn't-show survey email

Subject “feedback”, one line, sent to every no-show. It costs nothing and it is the only way to get the real reason rather than the guessed one. Feed the answers straight into the confirmation-page videos.

Mine sales-call transcripts for breakout video topics

He tells the room to dump every transcript into Claude Projects and pull the recurring objections out, then film one short video per objection and stack them under the urgency video.

Show an operational number as proof

His dispute rate is the single most credible thing in the funnel and it costs nothing to show. We have equivalents. The instinct to lead with student wins is not wrong, but it is the same thing every competitor does.

Offer Buy and Call at the same time

Two QR codes, one slide. A prospect who is already sold should never be forced through a call to pay.

Treat AI answers as a funnel step

Our own AI visibility audit came back 22/100. He frames that as a show-rate and close-rate leak, not a branding problem, and built one of five systems around it.

What we could not observe

The opt-in was never submitted — the form requires a phone number, and we do not fabricate one, so the email sequence and the Hammer Them SMS campaign are described by him on-slide but not captured in our own inbox. The $5,000 price is verified from on-slide testimonial screenshots and from the spoken transcript, not from a price slide or a checkout page; there is no checkout URL exposed anywhere in the bundle, only the two QR codes on the closing slide. Conversion numbers, registration volume and actual spend are all unknown — the only hard traffic fact is a 38-day Meta flight across three creatives.
